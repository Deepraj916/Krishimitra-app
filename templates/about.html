Our Mission

Krishimitra was born from a simple idea: to empower Indian farmers with the modern digital tools they need to thrive. In a world of rapidly advancing technology, we saw a gap between the potential of AI and data analytics and the day-to-day challenges faced by the agricultural community.

The Problems We Solve

  • Crop Loss: Millions of rupees are lost each year to preventable plant diseases. Our AI-powered Disease Detector provides an instant, accessible diagnosis, helping farmers take quick and effective action.
  • Market Access: The Agro Store provides a transparent, peer-to-peer marketplace for farmers to sell their products and for buyers to find authentic agricultural goods without unnecessary middlemen.
  • Information Gap: Access to timely and accurate market data is crucial. Our Live Market Prices feature, powered by official government data, gives farmers the information they need to make profitable decisions.

By integrating these powerful tools into one simple, accessible platform, Krishimitra aims to be a true "friend to the farmer," helping to build a more efficient, profitable, and sustainable agricultural future for India.
